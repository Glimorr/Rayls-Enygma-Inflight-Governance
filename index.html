01 / The question

What happens after acceptance, but before credit?

The source bank and Private Hub can accept a transfer before the destination bank has executed it. We introduced governance freezes during that interval and compared the result with matched unfrozen payments.

03 / The finding

Stopping execution is not the same as settling an obligation.

All three frozen cases encountered the Bank B CTS preflight frozen check; none produced a mined destination revert. Yet their already-recorded source burns were not automatically reversed in the observed workflow.

3.00Unreconciled source burns

Fresh read-only ERC-20 eth_call verification: combined balance and supply 17, compared with initial 20.

A freeze can prevent recipient execution and still leave a financial state that demands an explicit recovery or compensation decision.
Interpretation boundary: These three tokens were unreconciled in the observed local test. The experiment does not establish permanent loss or rule out an approved recovery mechanism.
04 / Reproduction and limitations

What the evidence supports.

Independent original research is useful only when the provenance and limitations are as clear as the outcome.

Verification

  • All six trial evidence files matched their original indexed SHA-256 digests.
  • Fresh RPC queries reauthenticated 15 canonical source, Hub and destination receipts.
  • Fresh eth_call checks confirmed final Bank A and Bank B balances and supplies.
  • All 18 freeze/unfreeze transactions were separately rechecked against their canonical blocks; the hub/source/destination chain genesis identities also matched.
  • A Bank B mint-simulation positive control showed expected frozen sensitivity. It was not a seventh transfer.
  • Forensic event-level verifier, eight tamper tests, independent live RPC checker and evidence checksums are provided.

Scientific limits

  • Only three observations per condition in an isolated local deployment.
  • The Hub-to-payment message join depends partly on independently captured native relayer reference records; cross-chain block heights are not a universal clock.
  • Documentation describes compensating reversals; an approved recovery procedure for these CTS pre-broadcast frozen cases remains untested.
  • Trial 2's source burn led to a prospective continuation-gate amendment before Trial 3.
  • A Trial 4 scanner false negative was corrected by independent canonical audit before Trial 5.
  • Frozen cases are CTS pre-broadcast failures—not mined destination reverts or refunded settlements.
  • The earlier original W5 environment, including its unresolved 10-unit payment, remains separate and was not restored by this study.
